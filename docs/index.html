Where Drex loses

Pricing goes to the LLM.

On 76 real eBay sold listings, Claude picked the closest price 43% of the time, Drex 30%, and a plain-code “3 closest comps” baseline 29%. Pricing needs world knowledge (a Supreme collab resells high). In round 1 Drex also scored 93% on negotiation, because it missed offers just under 90% of list. Once plain code did the arithmetic, it reached 100%.
